
# SCORE ERP & AI Integration — Executive Summary

A one-page overview of the full proposal in [degine.md](degine.md).

---

## The Goal

Replace SCORE's scattered spreadsheets and paper records with a single **ERP platform** (one source of truth for all departments), and layer **AI** on top to automate the slowest, most error-prone manual work.

---

## Task 1 — ERP

**Why an ERP?** Centralizes finance, beneficiaries, projects, HR, donors, and reporting — improving efficiency, data accuracy, transparency, compliance, and decision-making.

**Core modules:** Beneficiary Management · Finance & Grants · Project Management · Donor/CSR CRM · Reporting & MIS · HR & Volunteer Management.

**Recommendation:**
- **ERPNext / NGOERP** — open source, low cost, highly customizable, built for Indian compliance.
- **Dhwani RIS** — offline-first, ideal for rural field data collection with later sync.

---

## Task 2 — AI Integration

**The bottlenecks:** paper surveys → manual Excel entry → manual skill grouping → manual livelihood matching → generic financial literacy → untracked CSR funding.

**AI solutions mapped to each bottleneck:**

| Bottleneck | AI Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Paper surveys & manual entry | Voice-based data collection (Gujarati/Hindi, e.g. Bhashini) |
| Manual skill grouping | AI skill clustering |
| Livelihood matching & follow-up | WhatsApp follow-up chatbot |
| CSR funding & reporting | Automated CSR impact reporting |
| Financial literacy | Personalized financial guidance via voice/WhatsApp |

---

## Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Pilot voice-based data collection** in one village; measure time saved, accuracy, and adoption.
2. **Phase 2 — AI skill classification** trained on existing Excel data; auto-cluster skills and generate livelihood recommendations.
3. **Phase 3 — Reallocate staff** from data entry to community engagement, training, and programme delivery.

---

## Bottom Line

Start small with an open-source ERP plus a single-village AI voice pilot, validate the impact, then scale across all programmes.
